# Exploratory Data Analysis - Indian E-Commerce Customer Behavior

An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis project focused on understanding Indian e-commerce customer behavior, sales performance, product performance, and business opportunities using Python.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 25,000 e-commerce sessions to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify opportunities to improve business performance.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue and sales performance
- Customer behavior
- Product and category performance
- Payment preferences
- Cart abandonment
- Customer satisfaction
- Monthly sales trends
- Marketing channel performance

---

## Business Problem

The objective is to answer key business questions:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which payment methods are most preferred?
- How much cart abandonment occurs?
- What are the monthly revenue trends?
- How satisfied are customers?
- Which marketing channels perform better?
- What improvements can increase sales?
